# Greater Cincinnati Funeral Home Contact List — Infinity Map Outreach

Compiled 2026-07-29. ~70 funeral homes checked across Hamilton County (OH),
Northern Kentucky (Boone/Kenton/Campbell), and Butler/Warren/Clermont counties (OH).

**How this was built:** every email below was surfaced from the funeral home's own
website or a public business listing (chamber of commerce, county directory,
trade association), then re-verified with an exact-string search confirming the
address is genuinely published. No email was guessed or constructed from a
pattern. Direct page fetching was blocked in the research environment, so
verification relied on search-indexed copies of these pages — **spot-check an
address against the source URL before emailing.** Entries that could not be
double-confirmed are in the "verify before use" section.

Many small family-owned homes publish Gmail/Roadrunner/Fuse addresses rather
than domain email — that is normal in this industry, not a red flag.

## Sending checklist

1. **No link, no attachment** — the letter highlights the Infinity Map but the
   workbook goes out only after a home replies or calls (513) 850-3570. This
   keeps it from being copied and turns every response into a conversation.
2. Personalize each email (director's name, their community) — see the letter in
   `funeral-home-partnership-letter.md`.
3. Send individually in small daily batches, not one mass BCC.
4. Spot-check each address against its source before sending.
5. Honor any opt-out immediately.
