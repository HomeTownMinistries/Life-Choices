**Subject:** A free tool that makes arrangement conferences easier for the families you serve

Dear [Funeral Director's Name / the team at Funeral Home Name],

My name is Michael Myers, and I lead HomeTown Ministries International here in
the greater Cincinnati area. I'm writing because we've built something I believe
could genuinely help the families who walk through your doors — and I'd love to
put it in your hands at no cost.

You know better than anyone what it looks like when a family arrives to make
arrangements completely unprepared. No one wrote down Mom's Social Security
number, her mother's maiden name, or where the insurance policies are. No one
knows whether Dad wanted burial or cremation, which hymn mattered to him, or who
should be called first. Grieving families end up making dozens of decisions in
their hardest hours with none of the information in front of them — and your
staff carries the weight of walking them through it.

That's the gap we built **The Infinity Map** to close.

The Infinity Map is a comprehensive life-and-legacy planning workbook — our
tagline is *"Because love plans ahead."* In one organized document, a person
records everything their family will need:

- **Personal information** — the vital statistics required for the death certificate, pre-filled before it's ever needed
- **Family tree and key contacts** — attorney, insurance agent, pastor, physician
- **Medical directives** — living will, healthcare POA, end-of-life wishes
- **Financial overview** — accounts, policies, and where the records live
- **Funeral wishes** — burial or cremation, service preferences, music, readings
- **Asset distribution, and legacy & wisdom** — the letters and values they want to leave behind

When a family completes an Infinity Map ahead of time, they arrive at your
arrangement conference with the death-certificate information ready, the wishes
documented, and far fewer impossible decisions to make on the spot. It makes
your team's work smoother, shortens difficult conferences, and naturally opens
pre-need conversations — because a family that has filled out an Infinity Map
has already started planning.

**Here's the partnership I'd like to explore:**

1. **Free copies for your families.** We provide the Infinity Map at no charge —
   digitally and/or printed — for your lobby, arrangement rooms, aftercare
   packets, and pre-need seminars.
2. **Community events.** We're glad to co-host legacy-planning workshops with
   your staff for churches, senior communities, and civic groups in your area.
3. **A trusted local referral.** As a ministry, we're not selling anything —
   no fees, no commissions. We simply want every family in greater Cincinnati
   to be prepared before the hardest day comes.

You can view the Infinity Map here: **[link to the Infinity Map PDF]**

best serve your families.

Thank you for the compassionate work you do every day. It matters, and we'd be
honored to come alongside it.

## Sending notes

- **Personalize the first line** with the director's name and one specific detail
  (e.g., "your family has served Price Hill for four generations") — funeral
  homes are relationship businesses and generic blasts get deleted.
- **Send individually**, not as a mass BCC. A handful a day from your normal
  Gmail account reads as genuine and avoids spam filtering.
- **Follow up once** after about a week, ideally by phone — funeral directors
  answer their phones.
- Each message includes your real name, organization, and contact info, and is a
  one-time business inquiry to a publicly advertised business address — but if
  any home asks not to be contacted again, honor it immediately.
